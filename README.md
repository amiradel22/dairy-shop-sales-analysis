# dairy-shop-sales-analysis
Power BI project analyzing dairy shop sales, profit, products, customers, branches, discounts, and returns.

Project Overview
This project analyzes dairy shop sales data using Power BI to understand sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer behavior, and branch performance.

Business Problem

The business needs to understand its sales and profit performance across different products, customers, and branches to identify strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for improvement.

Project Objectives

Analyze sales and profit performance.

Identify the best-performing products and branches.

Understand customer purchasing patterns.

Evaluate discounts and returns.

Provide insights and recommendations to support better business decisions.

Tools Used

Power BI — Data analysis and visualization.

Microsoft Excel — Data storage and preparation.

DAX — Calculations and performance measures.

Key Business Questions

1.How do sales and profit change over time?

2.Which products generate the highest sales?

3.Which products generate the highest profit?

4.Which branches perform best in sales and profit?

5.Which customer types contribute most to sales and profit?

6.How do discounts affect sales and profitability?

7.Which branches have the highest number of returns?

Key Insights


1.Sales and profitability varied from month to month. March 2026 recorded the highest sales and profit, while December 2025 recorded the lowest sales and profit.

2.The difference in sales between December 2025 and March 2026 was mainly associated with a higher quantity sold, while customer count remained the same and average selling price increased slightly.

3.Full Cream Milk 2L recorded the highest total sales, while Mozzarella Cheese 500g generated the highest total profit. Strawberry Yogurt 170g had the highest profit margin among the listed products.

4.Downtown Flagship recorded the highest total sales, net sales, and profit among the three branches, while Oasis Express recorded the lowest values.

5.Downtown Flagship recorded the highest total profit, but it also had the highest number of returns, making returns an area worth further investigation.

6.Regular customers contributed the largest share of sales, net sales, and profit compared with VIP and Wholesale customers.

7.Differences in monthly net sales were not explained by invoice count alone. Average invoice value also varied across months and contributed to differences in net sales.

Recommendations

1.Investigate the reasons behind monthly sales fluctuations to identify opportunities to improve sales and profitability.

2.Focus on high-performing products while reviewing the performance of products with lower profitability.

3.Monitor branch performance and investigate why some branches generate higher sales and profit than others.

4.Analyze return patterns, especially at Downtown Flagship, to identify potential issues and reduce unnecessary losses.

5.Segment customers and review average invoice value to identify opportunities to increase sales and improve customer profitability.
